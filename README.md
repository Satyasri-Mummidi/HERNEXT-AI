# HerNext – Women’s Health Navigator 🌸

### From Health Confusion to the Right Next Step

HerNext is a multilingual, privacy-conscious GenAI health-navigation assistant designed to help women understand health concerns and confidently identify their appropriate next step.

> **HerNext is not a diagnostic tool. It provides health education and navigation support and encourages users to consult qualified healthcare professionals when needed.**

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Women often face difficulty understanding health information, especially when it is technical, confusing, contradictory, or difficult to access.

They may also struggle to understand:

- Whether a health concern requires attention
- What their symptoms or concerns may mean
- When they should consult a healthcare professional
- What questions to ask during a medical consultation
- When urgent professional care may be required

This can lead to confusion, anxiety, delayed care, or difficulty communicating with healthcare professionals.

---

## 💡 Our Solution

**HerNext** uses Generative AI to transform a user's health concern into clear, understandable and actionable health navigation.

### Core Journey

**Health Concern → Minimum Questions → Simple Explanation → Safety Check → Right Next Step → Doctor Preparation**

The application focuses on helping users make informed decisions rather than diagnosing medical conditions.

---

## ✨ Key Features

### 🧠 Understand My Concern
Converts complex health information into simple, easy-to-understand language.

### ❓ Minimum Necessary Questions
Asks only relevant follow-up questions instead of overwhelming users with unnecessary information.

### 🚦 Next-Step Navigation

- 🟢 General information / monitor
- 🟡 Routine healthcare consultation
- 🔴 Prompt or urgent professional care

### 🚨 Red-Flag Safety Check
Identifies potentially concerning situations and encourages appropriate professional or urgent medical attention.

### 👩‍⚕️ Healthcare Visit Preparation
Creates a concise consultation summary and generates useful questions to discuss with a healthcare professional.

### 🌐 Multilingual Support
Supports:
- English
- Telugu

Also provides simple-language explanations for users with different health-literacy levels.

### 🔐 Privacy-Conscious Design
Avoids collecting unnecessary personally identifiable information such as names, phone numbers, addresses, or government IDs.

## 🔄 How HerNext Works

1. User describes a health concern in their own words.
2. HerNext asks only the necessary follow-up questions.
3. The AI summarizes the concern in simple language.
4. It provides general health education without diagnosing.
5. A safety and red-flag check is performed.
6. The user receives an appropriate next-step recommendation.
7. HerNext generates questions for a healthcare professional.
8. A consultation summary is created for easier communication.

---

## 🎯 Target Users

HerNext is designed for women who:

- Have difficulty understanding medical information
- Need simple and accessible health education
- Are unsure whether a concern needs professional attention
- Want help preparing for a healthcare consultation
- Prefer communicating in their regional language

---

## 🔒 Safety & Responsible AI

HerNext follows a **health-navigation, not diagnosis** approach.

The application:

- Does not diagnose diseases
- Does not claim medical certainty
- Does not prescribe medication
- Does not replace healthcare professionals
- Does not encourage delaying urgent care
- Clearly communicates limitations
- Encourages professional medical consultation when appropriate
- Avoids unnecessary collection of personal information

---

## 🌟 What Makes HerNext Different?

Unlike a traditional symptom checker, HerNext focuses on the **next appropriate action**.

It helps users move from:

> **"I don't know whether this matters."**

to:

> **"I understand my concern, I know my next step, I know what to ask a healthcare professional, and I know when I need urgent help."**
